    Getting started

    Open a text file or paste a script from the start screen. The prompter saves it, works out how long it will take, and shows when each section starts. Press Start prompter, then Space.

    Writing a script

    Blank lineNew paragraph
    # HookSection heading, shown small with its start time, not read out
    [PAUSE] on its own lineStops until you press Space
    [PAUSE 2]Waits two seconds, then carries on
    //A breath mark, no stop
    [SMILE]Any other words in square brackets are a reminder, not read out
    *word*Emphasis

    Keys in the prompter

    Space or Page DownStart, pause, or carry on after a stop
    ↑ ↓Faster or slower (in clicker mode, previous or next line)
    ← or Page Up, →Previous or next paragraph
    Shift + ← →Previous or next section
    HomeBack to the start
    + - and [ ]Text size and column width
    KWord highlight: boxed, karaoke or off
    T M FColours, mirror, full screen
    V H SVoice follow, hide controls, settings
    EscPause, then back to the start screen

    A presentation clicker works too: forward starts and pauses, back goes up a paragraph. You can scroll with the mouse wheel, or click any paragraph to jump to it.

    Looking natural on camera

    Put the prompter at the top of the screen, right under the webcam, and keep the column narrow so your eyes barely move.

    Sit back a little further than usual. Bigger text from further away keeps your gaze steadier.

    Pick a natural pace, around 150 words a minute, and let the pauses do the work. Normal sentence case reads faster than capitals.
